#NPC #Magnolian_Sea #Dead-Gone 
Yhorm is the giant responsible for withstanding the collective pain and suffering of everyone who is [[Dying#The Damned|damned]]. He sits in his throne burning in green fire for all eternity. Finding him is a feat not achievable by many. He is hidden inside the deepest layers of the [[Star Tomb (Magnolia)]]. Here he guards the [[Star Tomb]] monolith, yearning desperately for the day [[Sir Gawain of Catarina]] will come to fulfill his promise.
# Post [[(Session 14) ...]]
Winning his life back in the [[Battle for the last breath]], Yhorm rose from the dead to reclaim his rightful throne. Not even [[Sombra de los Muertos]] himself could numb out the collective pain of all the [[Dying#The Damned|Damned]] souls. He successfully managed to fight off the last of [[Star Up]], so he made his way up to the [[Star Tomb (Magnolia)#4th Floor|Throne]]. Just as the shattering realisation of what had happened to the throne hit him, so did the [[Storm Ruler]]s final blow. Yhorms head fell to the floor, his crown rolling off it. A gust of wind blew past his headless corpse leaving nothing but his [[Star Fragment|Crystal]] skull.
# Notes
- Weak to [[Storm Ruler]].
- Killing him will result in the [[Dying#The Damned|damned]] getting more aggressive.
