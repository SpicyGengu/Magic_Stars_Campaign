#Lore #Recap 
[[Gawain's Island]] had now reviled it's true nature. From the [[Magnolian Sea|Magnolian Depths]] rose a rich and living jungle. This was most certainly where the [[Star Tomb (Magnolia)]] was located. Having teamed up with [[Sir Gawain of Catarina]], [[Star Up]] was now ready to explore what they came here for. Exploring the jungle, the party came across some ainchent ruines that led them down a long forgotten cave. They soon came to realise that this cave was filled with traps. Upon even closer inspection [[Grimshot]] realised the cave was haunted by a ghost. Avoiding the traps by the skin of thier teeth, the team learnt the power of ghost-bullets and [[Sombra (Calaca Sinclair)|Calaca]]'s bite. This cave proved to be deeper than initaly assumed, and at one point [[Sir Gawain of Catarina]] couldn't keep up and chose to stay behind. Our protagonists took the [[Storm Ruler]] and bid [[Sir Gawain of Catarina]] fair-well. Dwelling deeper into the tomb, the interior started to form structure.
