#Lore #Recap 
[[Gawain's Island]] had now reviled it's true nature. From the [[Magnolian Sea|Magnolian Depths]] rose a rich and living jungle. This was most certainly where the [[Star Tomb (Magnolia)]] was located. Having teamed up with [[Sir Gawain of Catarina]], [[Star Up]] was now ready to explore what they came here for. Exploring the jungle, the party came across some ainchent ruins that led them down a long forgotten cave. They soon came to realise that this cave was filled with traps. Upon even closer inspection [[Grimshot]] realised the cave was haunted by a ghost. Avoiding the traps by the skin of their teeth, the team learnt the power of ghost-bullets and [[Sombra (Calaca Sinclair)|Calaca]]'s bite. This cave proved to be deeper than initially assumed.  Riddled with secrets one even hiding the [[Ring of Oroboros]]. At one point [[Sir Gawain of Catarina]]s celestial form couldn't keep up, so he chose to stay behind. Our protagonists took the [[Storm Ruler]] and bid [[Sir Gawain of Catarina]] fair-well. Dwelling deeper into the tomb, the interior started to form structure. Faces started to take form inside the walls, then silhouettes, and then full statues. They were led to a big open room comprised of a wide platform standing toll upon the bottomless abyss. As if awoken by [[Star Up]]s presence, the abyss gave birth to it's protectors. Knights which battle tactics can only be described as rouge and relentless. Managing to fight the watchers off, our protagonists heroes managed to trap one last one in the middle of the room. A predicament he decided to solve by taking his own life. [[Grimshot]], [[Yalier Verdrak, Hope of The Hopeless|Yalier]] and [[Friendly]] soon realised this solution wasn't as irrational as it would first seem. They were contemplating it for a while. Even [[Sombra de los Muertos|Sombra]] decided to show face. Came to give some words of encouragement, so to speak. Feeling provoked by [[Sombra de los Muertos|Sombra]]s untimely appearance [[Sombra (Calaca Sinclair)|Calaca]] took it upon herself to summon [[Zina]], a figure which presence seemed to make [[Sombra de los Muertos]] moderately uncomfortable. This proved to be a great choice of action for [[Star Up]] since it even led to [[Sombra de los Muertos]] leaving. An eternity might as well have passed and our musketeers were no closer to getting out as when they started. In a sequence of events not even a God could have foreseen, [[Grimshot]] was the first to break. All signs led him down the same rode; the one carved by a bullet through his frontal cortex. Give a monkey a typewriter and he too will one day write Shakespeare, I suppose. The others followed suit and they managed to escape this banally evil predicament.
